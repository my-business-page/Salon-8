THE PRIVATE NAIL ROOM

Small details.
Big difference.

Maniqure By Ling is a home-based nail studio in Khatib, publicly known for gel manicures, creative nail art and decorative details.

01 / PRIVATE

A more personal setting

The studio operates from Khatib Central rather than as a conventional mall-style salon. Appointment details and access should be confirmed directly before your visit.

02 / DETAIL

Design is the focus

Public features highlight pastel gel sets, freehand nail art and playful character designs, alongside crystals, stones, studs and pearls.

03 / CHOICE

Build your own look

Start with a colour direction, then decide whether your set should stay minimal or build into art, charms and embellishments.

04 / REFERENCE

Bring inspiration

Reference photos are useful for communicating the exact mood, colours, shapes and level of detail you want. Send them before the appointment.
